#  Wood Clone Jutsu - Naruto Mod Addon (1.12.2)

A custom Forge addon for **AHZNB's Naruto Anime Mod (1.12.2)** that introduces the solid and durable **Wood Clone Jutsu** (*Mokuton: Moku Bunshin no Jutsu*).

---

##  Features

* **Dynamic Stat Scaling:** Inherits 80% Max HP, 60% Armor, and scales Attack Damage directly from summoner stats.
* **Combat AI & Jutsu Combos:** Casts *Wood Burial*, *Wood Arm Thrust*, heavy taijutsu strikes, and leap attacks.
* **Dual AI Modes:**
  * **Follow Mode:** Escorts the player, navigates terrain, and uses an emergency **Body Flicker Jutsu (Shunshin)** if separated by over 35 blocks or stuck for 6 seconds.
  * **Sentinel Mode:** Toggled via **Shift + Right-Click** with an empty hand; guards and patrols a 32-block radius, returning to its post when safe.
* **Canon Interactions:** Natural passive health regeneration, 1.5x fire damage vulnerability, and loyalty protection against summoner friendly-fire.

---

##  Requirements

* **Minecraft:** 1.12.2
* **Forge:** 14.23.5.2864+
* **Primary Mod:** [AHZNB's Naruto Mod](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ahznb-naruto-mod)
